# Professional sources and application boundaries

Accessed2026-11-01. Public lesson-note text was read, not the video lessons. No timestamps are claimed; no purchase, bypass, installation or external asset upload occurred. The material informs construction/diagnosis; Mia's stylized proportions remain governed by original art.

| Creator / original lesson | Confirmed public text | Concrete Mia hypothesis / limit |
|---|---|---|
| Steven Michael Hampton, [Focus on Features – The Eye](https://www.proko.com/course-lesson/focus-on-feature-the-eye) | Eyelids have planar/boxy structure and depth. Upper/lower turning points are asymmetric. The socket recedes medially and turns laterally; tissue bridges the inferior orbit to cheek. | Compare actual upper/lower runs and turns, lid top/bottom planes and ocular/socket depth. A rounded parallelogram is a design interpretation to verify against art. Do not copy adult-realistic eye proportions or carve a hollow cheek to fake depth. |
| Stan Prokopenko, [How to Draw the Rib Cage](https://www.proko.com/course-lesson/how-to-draw-the-rib-cage) | Construct one cage volume with front, side and top planes, upper narrowing, broader lower region and sternum/costal-arch landmarks. | Establish coherent torso cross-sections before local breast work. Illustrated body turnarounds are not exact skeletal blueprints; no rigid human-average dimensions are imposed. |
| Stan Prokopenko, [Common Mistakes of Drawing Breasts](https://www.proko.com/course-lesson/common-mistakes-of-drawing-breasts/) | Treating breasts as uniformly round rigid spheres and forced central contact is a common error; upper attachment tapers while lower mass responds to weight. Clothing changes appearance. | Remove unsupported upper-ball/lower-shelf transitions while retaining intended bust scale. Source bra support cannot directly define the unclothed fold; fixed separation/angle values are not universal targets. |
| Stan Prokopenko, [How to Draw Breasts – Form & Motion](https://www.proko.com/course-lesson/how-to-draw-breasts-form-and-motion/) | Breast mass transitions over the lower pectoral area toward the axilla on a curved cage; upper/lower profile planes differ. Shoulder/arm motion changes the mass and fold. | Reconstruct connected upper, medial, lateral and inferior transitions and inspect small shoulder probes. This does not constitute cloth/secondary-motion or full rig validation. |
