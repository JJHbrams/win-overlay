# A1 rejected before review

A1 is retained only as a reproducible failed probe. It was built from the face-producer F2 native rather than the requested integrated WIP8 baseline, and it copied a legacy protected-ID set without first locating those vertices in the evaluated mesh. Its broad Gaussian-style patch therefore does not meet AC1 and must not be integrated or used as evidence of an anatomy correction.

The source native and the A1 candidate remain unmodified after generation. `diagnose_wip8_surface.py` is the replacement read-only step.
