# A1 primary-form diagnosis

The F2 neutral face retains a narrow under-eye/nasal diagonal transition.  F2's recipe restored the lower cheek with height-section depth guides but intentionally protected the high malar region; therefore the upper orbital-to-malar-to-maxillary bridge remained structurally untouched. The visible result can read as a crease above a separate cheek volume.

This is a working hypothesis, not a facial measurement from cropped source art. A1 tests a wide, low-amplitude carrier-surface correction: lower orbital support into the lateral malar support and anterior maxillary cheek. It avoids the eye aperture boundary, nose, mouth/chin, outer seam, all non-skin objects, UVs, materials and the *relative* form of every existing face key.

Pass condition is not reduced contrast. In neutral clay, both obliques and low views must read as a continuous mass while the profile does not become a puffy sphere or lose Mia's jaw/nose relation. Native light and source boards are later checks, not substitutes for this geometry pass.
