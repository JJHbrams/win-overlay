# Rejected jaw hypotheses: Q10 and Q11

Both candidates are REJECTED. Root retains WIP9 geometry. No jaw completion or source-likeness acceptance is claimed. Original producer natives remain in their worktree for traceability; no native files are copied here.

Q10 used a frontal section envelope plus polynomial anterior-depth carrier. Actual bare matte shows new horizontal cheek/jaw folds, side-return concavity and a chin-adjacent spur. The target-to-boundary transfer failed to construct coherent 3D curvature.

Q11 was a separate X-only globally circumferential C2 mapping, with Y/Z exactly unchanged and a positive global Jacobian. Its frontal notch decreased, but new parallel lower-cheek grooves and a crumpled mouth-corner patch extended rearward in oblique/profile. Producer and independent reviewer agreed net regression.

Explicit lesson: X-only deformation and unchanged Y do not prevent normal-curvature grooves. A monotone/invertible mapping protects topology and coordinates, not bending quality or source-faithful appearance. Even a C2 displacement changes side-surface orientations and can concentrate curvature. Technical invariants and smaller silhouette notches cannot outweigh the actual rendered regression.

For each candidate this archive includes the exact recipe, delta NPZ, audit and rejection record; actual baseline/candidate matte front/left-oblique/profile images; original camera/key render records; and four source|baseline|candidate comparison boards with their original reference package. No additional coefficient variants were produced. No duplicate15-view image sets or giant natives are included.
