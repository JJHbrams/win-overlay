CAUSAL FINDINGS
1. The outer eye to cheek transition is genuinely abrupt in geometry. At world x=+55mm the evaluated surface normal turns 50.23 degrees across z1.542 to1.532 (10mm); front depth recedes 8.82mm across z1.542 to1.522. This is a local sampled fact, not a universal anatomical target. Both 45-degree neutral-clay views retain the broad orbital ledge and side-cheek wall without makeup. A toon normal ramp and under-eye paint reinforce it; they do not create the whole issue.
2. The face reads broad through the upper cheek and flatter through the lateral midface than the portrait. Hair-on frontal comparison also has a broader exposed face framing; side locks obscure the true art contour. Do not infer a precise percentage scale from these unmatched art poses. Measured frontal depth-gated widths: z1.520 ~107.81mm, z1.535 ~121.40mm, z1.550 ~133.85mm. These exclude much ear/rear skull but are not exact anatomical segmentation.
3. Bare cranium is not shown to be too short. Crown z1.645209, chin z1.466560; crown-to-eye about105.9mm, eye-to-chin about72.7mm. The frontal-parietal surface is wide/flat and the side profile has a large posterior mass. Upper35 hair-off views show substantial upper-head volume. Hair crest/silhouette is not a valid skull-height measurement. A global head-height increase is not supported by this evidence.
4. Neutral clay reveals a separate face/cap shading join above the temple/ear. This merits a boundary-normal/position continuity check if cranial work follows. The lower orbital-to-cheek turn is within the face skin and cannot be blamed entirely on that join.
5. Face:728 control vertices,1342 triangles,47 keys; Subsurf2 evaluates16338 vertices/16104 polygons. All source polygons smooth, no custom_normal attribute. Body/cap3906 control vertices ->63505 evaluated, Weld before Subsurf2 and local Smooth. Eye carrier436 vertices/544 polygons, no subdivision, no keys, custom normals present; active emissive eye appearance and mesh silhouette are not fixed by skin normal recalculation. Density is adequate for a static prototype but sparse, uneven triangulated control flow is not a mature deformation topology. More global subdivision would polish the existing shape, not correct its proportions or the ledge.
