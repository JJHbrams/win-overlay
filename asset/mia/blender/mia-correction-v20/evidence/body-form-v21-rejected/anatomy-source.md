# Artist anatomy source
Stan Prokopenko, How to Draw Breasts - Form & Motion, official Proko lesson.
https://www.proko.com/course-lesson/how-to-draw-breasts-form-and-motion/
Accessed 2026-10-31 through web open. Read public Lesson Notes Form and Motion paragraphs; did not watch video, access paid course or download reference photographs. No user/private human references used.

Applied principle (paraphrase): chest mass follows the rounded rib surface, with a soft transition toward the pectoral/armpit region; profile has upper and returning planes, and the lower return varies with mass and pose. This is a construction principle, not a required numeric anatomy or size for Mia. It supports distributing attachment rows across the rib surface while preserving the character's principal volume. Concentric loops are not inherently incorrect; the observed compressed returning band is the current form problem.
