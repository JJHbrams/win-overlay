## Apply to the active upper-body problem

Use the latest H plan and actual accepted surface. Preserve the existing wider
arm/waist transitions and accepted face while examining the entire upper torso.
Do not restart candidate grids, blind pelvis enlargement or head scaling.

Explain the connected structure before changing it: ribcage/chest-wall mass;
clavicle and scapular shoulder placement; pectoral transition and anterior
axillary fold; back/lat transition and posterior fold; the open passage between
them; supported breast mass on the chest wall. This is a working anatomical
hypothesis to check in Mia, not a geometry prescription proved by the catalogue.

Look at front/back for shoulder roof, neck flare and torso proportions; profile
for breast support/projection and chest depth; obliques/high/low for roots,
folds and connected transitions. Read hair-off when needed, then hair-on. Keep
arm pose consistent or explicitly account for it. A skin web across the axilla,
pinched arm root or broad shelf is not fixed by a softer shader.

When a fixed primitive/support floor prevents source-like shoulders, report
which constraint prevents the visible edit and use the authorized local domain.
Do not shrink unrelated breasts or defeat the preservation boundary. Positive
Jacobian, nested volumes and small residuals test particular properties, not
anatomical shape or source likeness. Direct local/cage editing may be preferable
to an underpowered field model; demonstrate improvement before selecting it.

Check supported underwear shape and plain-skin appearance separately on the same
declared geometry. Do not treat clothing compression as naked rest anatomy.
Temporary pose tests inform the shape; final animation/secondary motion remains
under its own acceptance criteria.
